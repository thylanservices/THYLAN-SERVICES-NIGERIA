About THYLAN

THYLAN SERVICES NIGERIA is a technology-driven engineering and ICT company dedicated to providing innovative, reliable and affordable solutions that improve security, productivity and sustainable energy across Nigeria.

Prince James Nsa

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Prince James Nsa is a visionary entrepreneur with academic backgrounds in Accounting, Business Administration and Computer Science. He founded THYLAN SERVICES NIGERIA with the mission of delivering world-class engineering and digital technology solutions to homes, businesses and institutions.

Under his leadership, THYLAN has continued to provide quality Solar Energy, CCTV Security, LAN Networking, Enterprise Software, Website Development and ICT Consultancy services throughout Nigeria.
